This is a real gem that prepares relatively quick and this dish awakens nice memories from warm and happy summer days. Sometimes (often?), it's the simple dishes that wins - don't over complicate stuff! At least, if you (like me) do it with ready made gnocchi (my choice is Rummo's Gnocchi di Patate 117) and passata (tomato purée). As for mozzarella I use julienne cut fior di latte, as it is already drained and it melts nice in the pot, together with the sauce. The parmigiano should be grated.


<img src="/assets/gnocchi_sorrentina_1.jpg" alt="Gnocchi Sorrentina Forno Legna.">

For 2-3 persons

|                          | Mängd       | prefix |             |
| ------------------------ | ----:       | ------ | ----------- |
| Gnocchi                  |   500       | g      |             |
| Passata                  |   600       | g      | Tomato purée|
| Mozzarella fior di latte |   300       | g      |             |
| Parmigiano Reggiano      |    20       | g      | Baking soda |
| Aglio                    |     2       | cloves | Garlic      |
| Sale                     |             | g      | Salt        |
| Pepe                     |             |        | Pepper      |
| Olio d'oliva             |             |        | Olive oil   |
| Basilico                 |             |        | Basil       |


Cover the bottom of a big pan with olive oil at meadium heat, add the whole garlic cloves and remove them just when they get blonde. Add the passata and let it cook to reduce in a controlled temperature. Meanwhile, put a big pan to boil. When the water boils, add two big pinches of salt into the water and add the gnocchi. Remove the gnocchi that floats to the surface and add them into the sauce. 
Power on the oven and set it to a rather high temperature (~275-350 degrees Celsius).
Chop a couple of basil leaves and add into the sauce. Add some black pepper and when the sauce thickened a bit, add the mozzarella. Taste of and add salt if needed and toss a couple of times so that the mozzarella blends into the sauce with some nice looking strings.

<img src="/assets/gnocchi_sorrentina_2.jpg" alt="Gnocchi Sorrentina Forno Electrico.">

Put the  mixture into an oven safe form, decorate with a couple of basil leaves and top it with the parmigiano.

My best attempt so far has been in my outdoor wood fired oven at a time when my kitchen oven were broke, and I tried to keep the air temperature below 350 degrees.
